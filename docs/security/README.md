# Security --- How Simba Protects Your Data

Your marketing data is encrypted, isolated, and protected by enterprise-grade infrastructure.

---

## Data Protection

- **Encryption at rest and in transit.** All data is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
- **Per-project access isolation.** Every dataset, model and result is scoped to its owner and the team it is shared with; no account can read another account's data.
- **Aggregated data only.** Simba works with channel spend, impressions, and business outcomes. No user-level or personally identifiable data is required.

---

## Account Security

- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** available for all accounts. It applies to every way of signing in, including Google and Microsoft.
- **Sign in with Google or Microsoft.** A provider identity is matched to your account only after you connect it yourself, from your profile or through a confirmation link we email to the account's address. Signing in with a provider never attaches it to an existing account on the strength of a matching email address.
